Neil Janwani

I am a second year Robotics PhD student at Georgia Tech with the Institute of Robotics and Intelligent Machines in the Dynamic Mobility Lab. My dream is to design personalizable robotic control that retains its performance and robustness while adapting to user preferences. My research generally focuses on imitation learning, multi-objective reinforcement learning, and preference-based learning.

I am supported by the NSF GRFP and IRIM fellowships. Previously, I completed my B.S. in Computer Science at Caltech, working with Prof. Joel Burdick and Prof. Aaron Ames on intention-aware safety filers and a soft ankle exoskeleton.
