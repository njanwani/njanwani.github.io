I am a Robotics PhD student at Georgia Tech IRIM in the Dynamic Mobility Lab. My research focuses on whole body control and personalization of humanoid and assistive wearable robots. I use motion imitation reinforcement learning (RL), multi-objective RL, and human-in-the-loop optimization to work towards this goal.

I am supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and IRIM Fellowship. Previously, I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from Caltech, where I worked with Prof. Joel Burdick and Prof. Aaron Ames on intention-aware safety filters and a soft robotic ankle exoskeleton.

Research

My research sits at the intersection of three core fields: humanoid whole-body motion imitation, multi-objective RL, and human-robot interaction. I have deployed my work on several hardware platforms, such as the Unitree G1 humanoid robot, the BRUCE humanoid robot, a custom robotic lower-limb exoskeleton, and more.
