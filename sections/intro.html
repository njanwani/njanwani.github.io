I am a Robotics PhD student at Georgia Tech IRIM in the Dynamic Mobility Lab. My research focuses on designing personalizable robotic control that retains performance and robustness while adapting to user preferences. I use imitation learning, multi-objective reinforcement learning, and preference-based learning to work towards this goal.

I am supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and IRIM Fellowship. Previously, I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from Caltech, where I worked with Prof. Joel Burdick and Prof. Aaron Ames on intention-aware safety filters and a soft robotic ankle exoskeleton.
