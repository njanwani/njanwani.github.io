A Learning-Based Framework for Safe Human-Robot Collaboration with Multiple Backup Control Barrier Functions
Neil Janwani*, Ersin Daş*, Thomas Touma, Skylar X. Wei, Tamas G. Molnar, Joel W. Burdick
IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2024

arXiv | abstract We estimate operator intention with an LSTM-based network to safely select among multiple safety filters—each defined by a different backup strategy—allowing behaviors that better align with operator intent while maintaining safety. We validate the approach through simulations and hardware experiments with a collaborative robotic manipulator.
